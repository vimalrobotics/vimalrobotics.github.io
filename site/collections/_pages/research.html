Peer-Reviewed Publications

Stormbreaker: An Assistive Autonomous Towing Robot
IEEE Conference Publication, 2023
View on IEEE Xplore  |  View Project →

This work presents the design and real-world deployment of an assistive autonomous robotic platform capable of safely towing a wheelchair. The system integrates ROS-based navigation, multi-sensor fusion, human-aware safety constraints, and reliability-driven control architectures to operate in dynamic human environments.

Design Patents

Adaptive End-of-Arm Tooling (Bio-inspired Compliant Gripper)
Registered Design Patent, Government of India
Design No: 370051-001 (Class 08-08)
Registration Date: 29 August 2022
View Project →

This patented design covers a hybrid adaptive gripper combining Fin Ray-inspired compliant fingers with vacuum-based grasping. The mechanism enables stiffness modulation for both delicate object handling and load-stable manipulation using a single end-effector.

Current Research Direction

My current focus lies in bridging academic robotics research with deployable, real-world systems. I am particularly interested in translating concepts such as compliance, safety-aware autonomy, and mechanical intelligence into robust, maintainable robotic platforms suitable for long-term human interaction.
